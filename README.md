### About me

I'm an AI Engineer and Backend Engineer based in Yerevan, Armenia, working remotely. For about 4 years I've been shipping production LLM systems (RAG, agents, MCP) and the Django, DRF and FastAPI services behind them.

- 🤖 Building agentic AI with LangChain and LangGraph: tool calling, RAG Q&A and retrieval over vector stores
- 🏗️ Designing backends for multi-role products: service layers, auth and RBAC, OTP and JWT sessions, Celery and Redis pipelines
- 🐳 Shipping with Docker Compose, PostgreSQL, Nginx, OpenAPI contracts, pytest and CI/CD
- 🎓 M.Sc. in Computer Science, Allameh Tabataba'i University (2026)
- 📫 Open to AI and backend engineering roles. Reach me at **imansharei.b@gmail.com**
